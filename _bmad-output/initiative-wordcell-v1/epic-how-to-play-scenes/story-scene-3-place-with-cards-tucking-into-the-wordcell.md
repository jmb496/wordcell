# Scene 3: place with cards tucking into the WordCell

## Description

Place scene laid out exactly like the game. The word's cards animate into the chosen WordCell leftmost first, each disappearing below the card above it, so the WordCell ends with one top card, not a stack; order dragging of tile D stays.

- Current state: scene id `place` was built in 8.3: `src/ui/scenes/place.ts`, `fixtures/how-to-play-place.json`. Layout is the 8.6/8.7 whole-play-area stage (`Scene.svelte` place mode); no new layout. Fixture commands, steps up to the Confirm tap (command 7) and frames 0 to 4 stay unchanged. Changed: the Confirm fly, which today slides the single `strip-4` block (the whole strip) to cell 6, and the final frame, which draws cell 6 as six cards (`cellCards`) and becomes one top card.
- Order dragging stays: tile D is dragged to the front (command 6; order D B A L K E, R-51); the free letter is L from cell 6 (R-41, R-60, Q-02).
- Tuck: the fly step stays step 6 (`place-step-6`; `rest` stays 6 x `flyToCell`). Step 6 drops `flies` and its `reveal.hide`; it gains `Step.tucks` (`cell`, `letters` in placement order). `defineScene` throws (AGENTS rule 6) when `cell` is not the last `placeOrder` frame's `placeTarget` or `letters` differ from its `placeOrder`. In `effects.ts` the `fly` case handles `step.tucks` before the carry/layout check and ignores step 6's `times`. Each `tuck-k` (k 0 to 5, D B A L K E, R-51) lasts 6 multiples of `duration('flyToCell')` (Animation speed applies, HP-2): card k flies in slice k of six. Plumbing: `Effect` gains optional `easing` (default `'ease-in-out'`); `PlayerDeps.animate` in `player.ts` gains an `easing` argument, `ScenePlayer.play()` forwards `e.easing`, `animate` in `Scene.svelte` passes it, and the test fakes of `PlayerDeps` follow. `tuck-k` sets `'linear'` and keyframes `[{offset 0, start}, {offset k/6, start, easing 'ease-in-out'}, {offset (k+1)/6, end}, {offset 1, end}]`; card k-1 lands as card k starts.
- Flier geometry: each flier is a `Card place="tray" mirror="static"` (no `data-card-id`/`data-testid`, AD-14) in a `tuck-k` div at left 0, top 0, `transform-origin: 0 0`, with `--wc-w` `RICH_TILE_W` (36) and `--wc-slot` `RICH_SLOT` (40) as in `.tiles`, so it starts as the strip tile. The `tuck` layer sits (motion on only), in the frame of the `place-<i>` layers (top `TRAY_TOP`), transparent, DOM-after them, fliers in order D to E, so card k paints above card k-1 and `tuck` above `place-5`. Tile and column Card have the same height (`--wc-H`) and face margin (a new `FACE_MARGIN` 2 in `geometry.ts`) but faces 36 and 40 wide, so only x scales: sx = `CARD_W / RICH_TILE_W`, sy = 1. Start `translate(stripTileX(k)px, STRIP_TOPpx) scale(1, 1)`; end `translate((placeCellX(cell) + FACE_MARGIN - FACE_MARGIN * sx)px, PLACE_CELL_TOPpx) scale(sx, 1)` so each flier's face covers cell 6's top-card face and the previous flier exactly. The build corrects the `MAX_CELL_CARDS` comment in `geometry.ts` (value stays 6).
- Effects and hand-off: new ids `tuck`, `tuck-0` to `tuck-5` and `confirm` (marked only on the `place-4` layer's Confirm span) join `placeScene.elements`. Phase 0: `tuck-0..5`; `tuck` (base opacity 0, opacity 1 for the six multiples); `strip-4`, `confirm` and the ghost each fade opacity 1 to 0 as phase-0 effects of `times: 1` `flyToCell`, while the fliers start at full opacity on the tile slots, so no tile shows twice and the ghost does not sit over empty space. Phase 1 (`kind: 'overlay'`, times 1, fill forwards): the swap is `place-5` keyframes `[{opacity 1}, {opacity 1}]` and `tuck` `[{opacity 0}, {opacity 0}]`, not the reveal's 0-to-1 fade, so E never dips; the lit and target outlines of frame 4 pop off at the swap (frame 5 has none). Every `place-<i>` layer redraws cell 6 from the fixture start (L), so L stays under the fliers; there is no `cell6` element. All per-card state is WAAPI keyframes (no runtime inline style), so tap-restart, Replay, Previous and the loop restore frame 4's look (Pause freezes).
- Effects and hand-off: new ids `tuck`, `tuck-0` to `tuck-5` and `confirm` (marked only on the `place-4` layer's Confirm span) join `placeScene.elements`.
