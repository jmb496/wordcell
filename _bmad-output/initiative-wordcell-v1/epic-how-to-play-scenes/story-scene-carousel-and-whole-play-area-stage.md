# Scene carousel and whole play-area stage

## Description

Replaces the stacked scene list in the How to play panel with a carousel, amending HP-1 and superseding HP-7's panel layout for the scenes only; the owner decision of 2026-10-17 overrides only HP-1's "no other visible buttons". Owns the carousel and the shared play-area stage; interface: `src/ui/scenes/` (carousel, stage), `HowToPlayPanel.svelte`, `Panel.svelte` (its shared `.content` gets the gutter; other panels' baselines may shift), the `text.ts` howTo control names.

- One scene at a time. Only the current scene is mounted: exactly one scene figure is in the DOM, keeping HP-6's `<figure>` with text description and `aria-hidden` animation. The text sections below are unchanged. No swipe or drag (buttons and keys only).
- Controls: previous and next arrow buttons (native, in `act()`, 44 px targets) and dots as buttons that jump to a scene. A disabled end uses `aria-disabled="true"` plus a dimmed style; its click is a no-op and focus is kept. No wrap-around. The carousel root is the container of arrows, dots and the scene (including its Next-frame/Replay and pause controls). Left and Right act only while focus is inside the root, via an element-level `onkeydown` on it (no window listener), go through `act()`, stop at the ends, call `preventDefault` and stop propagation when handled (also at the ends) and ignore modified keys. Focus stays on the activated carousel control (arrow, dot); whenever focus was inside a scene that unmounts or re-enters (scene change, refit, reduced-motion toggle) it moves to the carousel root (`tabindex="-1"`), never body, except that when Next becomes Replay at the last frame (`{#if frame < last}` in `Scene.svelte`, unmounting Next) the build moves focus to Replay. Game keys are already suspended while the panel is open (`SUSPENDING` in `src/ui/overlays.svelte.ts`, checked by `attempt()` in `src/ui/keys.ts`).
- Names in `text.ts` howTo: "Previous scene", "Next scene"; dots "Scene n of N" (N = scene count) with `aria-current="true"` on the current one. Names plus `aria-current` are the announced position (no live region). Dots have a 44 px hit area; the row wraps to at most two rows.
- The scene is keyed on (index, reduced motion, Animation speed, fitted card width), so a change of any re-enters it as a newly shown scene: step 1, playing; under reduced motion frame 1 per the existing Scene behaviour. The carousel index is component state (not `overlays.svelte.ts`) and resets to scene 1 when the open flag goes false. A tap on the current dot is a no-op. HP-1's tap-to-pause/restart, small pause/play icon and loop-with-rest carry over on the stage unchanged.
- Reduced motion keeps the existing `Scene.svelte` behaviour: a scene opens on frame 1, the Next-frame control steps and clamps at the final frame (the HP-2 resting state), and the existing Replay control resets to frame 1. A scene change resets them. "Next" (`sceneControls.next`) and "Next scene" collide under substring locators, so e2e names match exactly; the frame control's accessible name stays "Next" ("Next frame" is prose only).
- The carousel mounts a scene only while the howToPlay overlay's open flag (`src/ui/overlays.svelte.ts`) is true, not on `Panel` presence (`Panel.svelte` keeps children mounted while it animates out). While the scene is unmounted during the Panel exit the carousel keeps its stage height as an empty placeholder, and a scene starts only after the first non-zero width measurement. A playing scene stops at its next step boundary while the tab is hidden and restarts at step 1 once visible (`ScenePlayer` `hiddenStop` with `game.whenVisible`, via `document.hidden` in `Scene.svelte`; no new listener, AGENTS.md); a scene paused during the hidden wait stays paused until the next tap (`player.ts`).
- Stage: one shared stage draws the whole play area like the game: eight columns, the tray with the word strip, and WordCells 3 to 10, in the relative order of `BAND_ORDER` (`src/ui/layout/bands.ts`: columns, tray, labels with WordCells). Cards are `Card` with `mirror="static"` (none of `data-card-id`, `data-testid`, `data-place`, `data-mirror-of`); the stage is `aria-hidden`, outside FLIP, reads no GameView (AD-3, AD-14).
- Stage contract: each scene supplies a position (columns, tray/word strip, WordCell tops) over a shared filler default, so "more cards" is concrete: at least 5 cards visible in every column. Filler is added at the far (top) end of each column so tails and bottom cards keep their positions; filler letters come from a fixed deterministic sequence (a constant in `src/ui/scenes/`, no randomness, pinned by the stage unit test) so the baseline is stable; filler needs no `CardId`: static `Card` takes `id={0}` and its face from a letter (as `Scene.svelte` does), filler is keyed by render key, and it is in no fixture, so fixtures and the HP-5 engine replay stay unchanged. Existing scenes keep their scripted cards and tests; their scripted geometry (fixed `CARD_W` 40, `COLUMN_PITCH`, `STAGE_W`) is rescaled from the fitted card width. The interim carousel holds all existing scenes in their current order, so the Tricky heading goes; 8.7 to 8.11 later prune it to five.
- Fit: the stage reads its panel's content width (ResizeObserver/`clientWidth`, not the live game layout store) and feeds a new pure card-width function in `src/ui/layout/geometry.ts` beside `cardWidth` and reusing `cardMetrics(w)`. Inputs: panel content width, the visible panel height, and the scenes' maximum column cards and tray rows (so the worst-case stage height is computable); the width term is `floor(content / 8 - 4)` as in `cardWidth`. Output: card width and stage height, identical for every scene at a given width. The width is the `.inner` box's content width (`Panel.svelte`: 560 px border-box, 16 px padding, so at most 528 px; 328 px for a 360 px viewport), and `.content` gets `scrollbar-gutter: stable` so a scrollbar appearing does not change it. A refit is a no-op when the integer card width is unchanged, so it never restarts an idle scene. The result at 328 px is at least 32 px. The stage and controls sit in the panel's scrolling content; where the stage would exceed the visible panel height (landscape, desktop) card width is capped so it fits, but the 32 px floor wins: below it the panel scrolls and the controls stay reachable.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: `npm run test:all` and `npm run test:screens` are green. Playwright tests start with their HP id (HP-1, HP-2, HP-6), unit tests with an AD-n.

- Playwright android (P6): one scene figure mounted and playing; arrows, dots and Left/Right keys (after focusing a carousel control) move between scenes; ends are aria-disabled, a click is a no-op and focus is kept; names and `aria-current` asserted; reopening the panel resets to scene 1; a newly shown scene starts at step 1; tap on the current dot is a no-op; tap pause/restart works; reduced-motion Next frame/Replay, and a scene change resets Next frame; closed panel has no mounted scene and no animation whose target is inside the carousel exists right after close, and `document.getAnimations()` is empty once the exit settles; rapid switching leaves only the current scene's animations (`document.getAnimations()`); hidden tab re-enters a playing scene at step 1 after becoming visible; resize/rotation refits and two scenes have equal stage height. Exact role+name locators. Focus is asserted after an arrow, a dot, Next frame (including the step to Replay) and a resize after focusing Next frame. The hidden-tab test sets `document.hidden` and `visibilityState` together, dispatches `visibilitychange` from the test and restores them. Reduced motion (`page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion })`) and resize each re-enter the scene; an idle scene does not restart on a refit with an unchanged card width. Animation speed cannot change while the panel is open (Preferences cannot open over How to play, `LEGAL_TOPS`), so reopening the panel covers it. Landscape: arrows and dots reachable. Bounding boxes: card width at least 32 px, arrows and dots at least 44 px, dot row within the measured panel content width (at most two rows from distinct dot top coordinates, no horizontal overflow).
- Unit: stage structure only (8 columns, WordCells 3 to 10 and word strip in the game's layout order per DESIGN.md/AD-11, none of the four card attributes, at least 5 cards per column; scripted cards unchanged); `act()` wrapping; the pure card-width function (at least 32 px at 328 px content width, defined for narrower widths, a short visible height caps the width, the 32 px floor wins below it).
- Screens: android, reduced motion, the first scene at its final (resting) frame, reached by the test clicking Next frame until the control reports the end; this ticket regenerates the baseline in the Docker image. Per-scene final-frame baselines are completed by tickets 8.7 to 8.11, and later tickets regenerate the first-scene baseline.
