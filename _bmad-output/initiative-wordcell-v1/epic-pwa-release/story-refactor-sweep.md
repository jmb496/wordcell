# Refactor sweep

## Description

Closing cleanup of epic 7 before the owner's device read (CAP-8; the sweep runs before entry 11, epic Notes 2026-10-29): one table row per deferred finding of entries 1–9; the 4.6 "main.ts controller-registration order has no test" item (`device-checks.md` G9) fixed by a test or declined with a reason; the unit suite's warm median, a watch re-run on a Linux-filesystem checkout and the AD-18 margin re-measured; the sweep's edits stay inside item 3's boundary. Done checkpoint (tickets.toml entry 10): the build stops at `built`; Jared settles owner-call rows, then marks it done; the owner or orchestrator applies the fix tickets and entry 11 amendment.

## Scope

1. Deferred-findings table, in this ticket's plan, one row per finding (duplicates merged into one row citing every source). Columns: source file:line (every source), finding, live at `baseline_revision` (yes/no, file:line or test-name evidence), disposition. Sources: the 7.1–7.9 plans (`deferred` frontmatter, Residual risks, Review Triage Log rows with a defer verdict, or a reject verdict at low or medium severity (any tag format; `maybe-false` counts as low)); the `## Result` unapplied minors and open majors of `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/review-loop/7-1-build.md` … `7-9-build.md` (7-4-build.md's Result points at its Pass 1 minors); ticket `*.review-log.md` Results only where the matching plan left them open; each Worth knowing or Close-out docs statement naming an open item in the digest for 7.1–7.9. The plan names its grep and counts per source; a source with nothing live gets a "none" row naming the search. A "closed by <commit>" cite must cover every clause of the finding at `baseline_revision`; a cite that does not hold makes the row live.
2. Dispositions: fixed here (item 3's boundary, item 5's pass); closed by <commit>; declined, with the reason; fix ticket (technical default for a finding that needs a change outside item 3's editable set); owner call (functionality or UX only); declined: handed to entry 11, a close-out document sync (e.g. 7.8 plan `deferred` :23; digest: build-notes `VITE_TEST_HOOKS === '1'`, F8 static-server wording, F6 `block` wording, the AGENTS.md storage exception, AD-18 Size and `allowJs`), citing the tickets.toml entry 11 or SPEC CAP-9 clause that covers it; rows no clause names (e.g. build-notes.md, SPEC F6 and F8 text) go in one plan group, for which the plan proposes an amendment to tickets.toml entry 11 (documents and clauses to add). The build never edits tickets.toml or the epic file: a fix-ticket row proposes the entry (title, finding, files, reproduction test), and the plan the entry 11 amendment, for the owner or orchestrator to apply before entry 11 starts, which then waits on the fix tickets. A disposition may go per clause; a clause asking for done-plan text goes to item 3's declined errata group.
