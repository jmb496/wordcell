# Deflake the helpers.spec.ts doubleTap test under load

## Description

Fix ticket from 7.10's sweep (row D-71, B8): the test `AD-17 doubleTap gives two touch taps whose clicks have detail 1 then 2, even 600 ms apart in real time` (`e2e/helpers.spec.ts`, identified by title, not line) failed once under full-suite load in `doubleTap`'s `[1, 2]` poll (before the test's < 400 ms check): click details `[1, 1]`. The stamp premise (item 2, the claim under test) normally holds, as the test's < 400 ms check after a 600 ms real wait shows; the build answers why, under load, the second tap's events landed ~1.7 s after its stamp (the first tap's matched its stamp, taken at dispatch; item 3). Related history only: D-64's mechanism (unstamped taps past Chromium's tap-count window); a fix here may touch D-64's path but does not close D-64 (SPEC Non-goals A7).

Sightings: bbdd2d5's final `test:all` (item 3); since then 30/30 alone; 0/3 counted with a green final `test:all` at 2d01200 and f29b440 (7.10 review passes 2, 3) and 84df83f (7.13 plan). Everything is recorded in this ticket's plan (`story-deflake-the-helpers-spec-ts-doubletap-test-under-load-plan.md`), citing D-71; the done 7.10 plan and its D-71 row stay unedited.
