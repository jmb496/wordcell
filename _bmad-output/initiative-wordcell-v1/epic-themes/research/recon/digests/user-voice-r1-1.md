## What the data suggests (inference)

- The recurring complaint is low contrast between tile, background and text states, especially in dark UIs. Players ask for adjustability, not specific art styles.
- Intense effects (scanlines) divide a small group, and the fix players accept is a slider or off switch.
- Colour variants need a non-colour cue.
