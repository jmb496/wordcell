- R7 boundary: the whole `describe('score history store')` block moves (build-notes.md CAP-10); the five game-store blocks stay; history-only helpers move; shared ones are copied (tests never import each other), unused leftovers deleted, named in the plan.
- Re-measure (AD-17), once after all in-scope changes, the median in the /mnt/d checkout: metric: Vitest-reported `Duration` of `npm test`, warm `fsModuleCache`, median of three after a discarded warm-up, wall clock beside it. A watch re-run median of 1.00 s or more records per-test timings and stops likewise (no split; CAP-10).
- Split shape: read Scaffold deltas first; three root-level `src/architecture-<topic>.test.ts` files of whole describe blocks: the AD-1 tree scan alone, the other AD-1 blocks, and the AD-2, AD-12 and AD-17 blocks; shared code in one root module, `src/architecture-scan.ts`; `SELF`, tsconfig.arch.json `files` and tsconfig.app.json `exclude` each become all of them plus that module; the plan records the grouping, per-file times after the split and the spine and AGENTS.md lines naming `src/architecture.test.ts` for the retrospective.
- Flakes: fix D1 and D4 first; a failed timing run is discarded and rerun; any test failing twice stops for the owner (D1).
- Interface: new src/shell/stored-key.ts and src/shell/history.svelte.test.ts; no export change outside src/shell/ (else a plan deviation); no src/engine/index.ts change; test-only: e2e/helpers/ modules (D8), split files, src/architecture-scan.ts.
- Tests: existing suites plus the inventory's added cases; no new rule coverage.
- Owns: R6, R7, the re-measure and split, the inventory below, and recording in the plan the AGENTS.md Where-things-are update (stored-key.ts, layout/, gestures/, act.ts, split files, architecture-scan.ts) for a bmad-project-context refresh; the build does not edit AGENTS.md (tickets.toml over build-notes.md CAP-10's "Update AGENTS.md").
