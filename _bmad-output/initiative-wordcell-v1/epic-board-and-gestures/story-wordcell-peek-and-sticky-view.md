# WordCell peek and sticky view

## Description

Adds WordCellView.svelte and its controller paths (R-65, Q-21, F14): a long-press ≥ 400 ms on a non-empty WordCell opens the peek through overlays.openPeek (no entry), closing on release unless the rendered sheet's scroll area overflows, when peekToSticky pushes one entry above any kept selection; WordCellView marks its scroll area with a data attribute, and at release on a long-press the controller queries it under its root and calls peekToSticky() if scrollHeight > clientHeight, else closePeek() (proven by P4); any other press end (incl. a same-id pointerdown) calls closePeek(), never sticky; an overflowing long-press peek turns sticky on release in every phase; a tap in Idle with nothing selected or in Game over opens the sticky view via openView (one entry); the peek and the sticky view render the same WordCellView sheet (DESIGN.md view sheet), titled WordCell n · m cards / WordCell n · 1 card from text.ts with m = cards.length (F27) over the scrim, listing CellView.cards bottom first with top on the last as mirrors (a used cell's top a normal-card mirror), and peekToSticky changes only its mode and entry; the sheet and its mirrors pan-y, user-select: none and -webkit-touch-callout: none with overscroll-behavior contain, a touch-action none scrim, the controller classifying sheet and scrim presses (tap closes and swallows that gesture's trailing click with a one-shot capture-phase click listener until it, the next pointerdown or a 300 ms expiry armed at pointerup; drag does nothing), back closing it; the swallower is a module in src/ui/gestures/, its listener on the controller's attach root calling stopPropagation() and preventDefault(), disarmed (timer and listener) by detach, using the controller's setTimer/clearTimer, unit-tested in node with an injected EventTarget and fake timers; while a sticky view is open its full-viewport scrim sits above both bars, banner and board (DESIGN.md layer order), so Undo, Redo, banner Reload, column and WordCell pointer presses cannot reach their handlers; Board.svelte moves the `--wc-*` properties to a wrapper holding `.board` (bars and banner inside) then WordCellView (sheet and scrim), within the controller's attach root (App.svelte:64 `<main use:pointerRoot>`); while open `inert` goes on `.board` only, never the wrapper, `<main>` or a view ancestor (else the controller misses sheet/scrim presses and the swallower); only the sheet/scrim path, back and hide run; a sheet or scrim tap only closes the view (never the F25 outside-tap deselect: a kept selection and its entry stay), and with a peek turned sticky above a kept selection (wordcellView above tailSelection, the overlays.deselect() hazard tickets 4.7 and 4.9 deferred here) the controller's cancel() and the hide flush (F24) do not throw and keep the selection and sticky view (entry depth unchanged, no nav call); Composing and Place WordCell taps and drags dispatch nothing and open no view while the peek works; in Idle with a tail selected a WordCell tap deselects with no view (R-14, existing) and a WordCell drag does nothing and keeps the selection; an empty WordCell opens nothing; a WordCell drag never lifts a tail (R-12); and the scripted valid fixture session-gave-up-after-commit.json (as session-idle-empty-column with gaveUp true) added to src/engine/serialize.test.ts (ValidName, VALID, PROPERTIES with its defining property gaveUp true, column 1 empty, cell 7 holds 7 cards, and REBUILDS with session-idle-empty-column's script plus give-up; the "The ten …" comment follows the count) and src/architecture.test.ts's valid session-fixture list and its "exactly the ten rebuilt ones" test name (count ten → eleven).

- Interface: new src/ui/WordCellView.svelte; text.ts gains the view title strings (F27); controller long-press, sheet and scrim paths; new fixtures/session-gave-up-after-commit.json.
- Tests: R-65 (UI) every card of any WordCell viewable at any time (peek, sticky, overflow, scrim cases), viewing changes nothing, the view does not enter the move history; Q-21 long-press under R-65; R-12 (P4 supp.); R-33 a Composing WordCell tap is a no-op in epic 4 (P4 supp.); R-39 (UI) Place WordCells inert except viewing (P4 part); R-75 viewing in Game over (P4 supp.); AD-13 sticky-view reload then back leaves the app; AD-13 peek/sticky entry counts (P4); AD-12 trailing click swallowed then a later click reaches its handler, disarm by pointerdown or expiry (S).
- Updated tests: e2e/tap.spec.ts:176 drops wordcell-7 (keeps empty wordcell-3); controller.test.ts:920 and intent.test.ts:66 re-pointed to the peek/open-view outcomes, other cases kept; the AC peek-with-tail-selected case extends e2e/tap.spec.ts:357 (peek visible while held, gone after release).
- Owns: WordCellView.svelte, the view title strings, session-gave-up-after-commit.json and the scrim click swallower.
