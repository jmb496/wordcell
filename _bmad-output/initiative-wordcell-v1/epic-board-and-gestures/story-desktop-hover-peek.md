# Desktop hover peek

## Description

Adds HoverPeek.svelte (F19) for (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) with pointerType mouse: hovering a non-empty WordCell 400 ms opens it through the overlays hover-peek state above the cell, clamped into the board column, below it when there is no room (dev fallback orders), never covering the cell; it shows the top 14 cards, oldest of them first, top card last and tagged top, as mirrors, a +1 older card / +n older cards line from text.ts above them only above 14 cards (F27), no scrim, takes no clicks, closes on leave, never opens during a drag or with a peek or view open, and any press cancels the timer and closes an open hover peek only when it starts a drag, a peek or the sticky view.

Interface: new src/ui/HoverPeek.svelte; text.ts gains olderCards(n) (F27); a controller pointerover timer for mouse input. Tests: R-65 (UI) hover peek on desktop (P4, desktop project); AD-13 openPeek closes a hover peek, hover then hold → only the peek sheet (P4 desktop + S from entry 5). Owns: HoverPeek.svelte and the older-cards strings.

Timer (controller, mouse only): it arms only for pointerType 'mouse' while layout's hoverFine ((hover: hover) and (pointer: fine), layout.svelte.ts, exposed as a getter) is true, read through ControllerDeps so tests stub it. An entry is a root pointerover whose relatedTarget is outside that cell's [data-wordcell] element (badge and hidden cards inside; the label outside), arriving while no press is held (pointerdown to that pointer's end) and no fly-back runs; leave is a root pointerout whose relatedTarget is outside it. The 400 ms timer arms once per entry into a non-empty WordCell; leave clears it and closes the hover peek; moving straight to another cell closes the old one and arms for the new one. Any press end (including a drag released over a cell), fly-back end, peek or view close or cancel() suppresses the [data-wordcell] under the last mouse point: pointerovers into it (boundary ones from capture release or scrim removal included) never arm until a pointerout leaves it. At fire it re-checks against the current GameView that the cell is non-empty and relies on the store gate (overlays.svelte.ts openHoverPeek: no open while a peek, the view or a modal top (history notice, reset confirm, expanded end sheet) is open), so "with a peek or view open" above includes those modal tops. cancel() and detach clear the timer and call closeHoverPeek() (AGENTS.md cancel-first; EXPERIENCE.md key commands close an open hover peek).

"Starts a drag": any press of any pointer type cancels the timer; any press crossing the drag threshold (inert, R-12) calls closeHoverPeek(); a press that becomes a peek or the sticky view closes it through the store (openPeek, peekToSticky, openView clear it); a non-primary-button pointerdown is no press (controller drops it): it cancels, blocks, suppresses and closes nothing; and any other press (e.g. a tap that does nothing in Composing or Place) keeps it open until leave.

Layout (DESIGN.md View sheet, Layer order): the view sheet's surface ({colors.surface}, {rounded.lg}, 16 px padding, cards in w + 4 slots with 4 px gaps as WordCellView.svelte), no title, no open/close transition this epic (deferred like the view sheet's, SPEC Non-goals); content-sized width min(count shown, 7)·(w + 4) − 4 + 32 px; cards at card size w, 7 per row, at most two rows; the older-cards line is a ui-label above the first row (n = count − 14); layered above the sticky bars and selection/lift and below the drag layer (DESIGN.md Layer order): .board-root gets position: relative, the HoverPeek root z-index 4 (sticky bars 2, lifted face 3 in Card.svelte, drag layer 5 in Column.svelte). The anchor box is the union of the cell and its number label (F25 labelsRow): above means the hover peek's bottom at or above the label's top; below means its top at or below the cell's bottom. "No room" means its height (measured on the rendered root after mount) exceeds the anchor box's top minus the board's top (board top = the .board top; board column = the --wc-board / metrics.board-wide band column centred in .board, not the full-width Board root); it then goes below, never clamped vertically (not intersecting the anchor box wins over staying inside the board; the page scrolls to show overflow). Centred on the cell's horizontal centre, then clamped into the board column's horizontal extent; positioned absolutely within the Board root in page-scroll content, so it moves with the cell on scroll and may overlap the sticky bars in short windows, drawn over them (no viewport clamp); re-placed on any layout change (w, band heights) while open.

Mirrors are drawn from CellView.cards (SPEC CAP-9): on a used cell the top (live in the tray or strip) is a normal-card mirror, unhatched, still tagged top (text.ts viewTop); mirrors carry data-mirror-of only and never animate (AD-14).

Locator: no data-testid (AGENTS.md); the HoverPeek root carries a `data-hover-peek` attribute and aria-hidden="true" (the sticky view stays the accessible path, R-65) (precedent `data-view-scroll`, no new catalogue string); tests locate it, and its `[data-mirror-of]` children through it. "Only the peek sheet visible" means zero `[data-hover-peek]` and the peek dialog visible.
