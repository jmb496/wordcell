# WordCell row place preview

## Description

Renders `cell.preview` in Place in `WordCellRow.svelte`: no `.used` mirror in any WordCell; a used non-target cell shows its live card beneath (or the empty outline); the target hides its current top and shows a new `preview` mirror kind in `Card.svelte` (`data-mirror-of`, a `preview` class on the wrapper as `used` sets `used`, no card id, no FLIP) of `cell.preview.top` (the strip's last card; on `session-place-free-letter-redo-tail` used target 3 shows a `preview` mirror of its own free letter 28 with the `★`, E2, R-41); a changed cell's shown top, empty outline (`preview.top` null), count badge (`preview.count >= 2`, DESIGN.md WordCell) and name come only from `cell.preview`, never `place.placementOrder` or `cell.cards.length`; `cell.cards` still supplies its live elements (hidden unless one is `preview.top`) and the R-65 view; a used card gets no element in its cell (AD-14); the `★` pill is a `Card.svelte` prop (e.g. `previewMark`) drawn inside the card rect at bottom-left, set by WordCellRow on the shown top of a non-empty preview (the live card beneath or the `preview` mirror) and by entry 4 on a `static` card, under the cell's 35 % dim, over the card flourish without suppressing it (as the count badge does; the `bandMark` rule is unchanged); in Place a changed cell exposes no named card button (the card beneath stays silent, the `preview` mirror is unnamed, the hidden current top is not exposed) and its group's `, after this word` name (the two in `src/ui/text.ts`) carries it; Composing, the R-65 view and the not-legal dim unchanged; adds `fixtures/session-place-used-cells.json` (seeded through `e2e/helpers/seed.ts`; a used single-card cell, at or below the word's letter count, and a used cell of at least 3 cards numbered above it (not legal by R-40, so its preview card, `★` and badge show under the 35 % dim), neither the target), registered in the AD-17 valid-fixture list (`src/architecture-ad2-ad12-ad17.test.ts`), `src/engine/serialize.test.ts` `VALID`, `PROPERTIES` (its defining property) and `REBUILDS` (a scripted rebuild), and ticket 11.1's Q-49 property loop in `src/engine/view.test.ts`; keeps `e2e/fly-to-cell.spec.ts` green: its Place start checks move to the preview, and the `flip.ts` capture skips the `preview` mirror (`[data-mirror-of]:not(.preview)`), so until entry 3 a used target's in-flight hold draws no used mirror (the existing hold shows the card beneath, WordCellRow's current `lowest − 1` rule); this ticket updates the F28 in-flight checks of the held `m28` used mirror (reduced motion included), the Undo-back-to-Place `[data-wordcell] [data-mirror-of]` count and the `src/ui/flip.test.ts` fake capture selector (both to `[data-mirror-of]:not(.preview)`) to that interim, and entry 3 rewrites them for the final hold (the interim lives only on the epic branch).

This ticket has a plan checkpoint: an unattended build stops after planning for a person to approve the plan before implementing.
